# Release 1.5 — Seed masters / on-demand history

**Status:** Outline — freeze when 1.4 ships  
**Milestone:** `1.5`  
**Parent:** [Nivesh_Product_Requirements_and_Roadmap_v1.2.md](../Nivesh_Product_Requirements_and_Roadmap_v1.2.md)

## Outcome

India stock and fund **masters** load for a defined universe without duplicate identity rows; heavy history stays on-demand sync.

## Foundation

- [ ] Master upserts are idempotent
- [ ] Seed failures log clearly

## Backlog

- [ ] Document India universe and seed commands (stocks, funds, indexes)
- [ ] Setup/guide defaults stay small (indexes + limited masters; not full history)
- [ ] Re-running masters seed does not duplicate schemes/companies
- [ ] History (NAV/prices/statements) remains UI/job sync after masters exist

## Out of scope

Full `max` history for every symbol on install; multi-country masters.

## Exit

Masters load cleanly twice with no dupes; opening a symbol still loads history via sync.

## Idea map

Seed data for each stock or mutual fund.
